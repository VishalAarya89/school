🏫 About School

Saraswati Shishu Vidya Mandir, Manua Ismailpur, Hajipur (Vaishali) ek pratishthit shikshan sansthan hai jo bachchon ko quality education ke saath-saath sanskar aur discipline dene ke liye jana jata hai.

🎯 Our Vision

Vidyarthiyon ko gyanwan, atmnirbhar, anushasit aur rashtra-nirman me yogdan dene wala nagrik banana.

🚀 Our Mission

Modern education ke saath Bharatiya sanskriti aur naitik moolyon ka vikas karna.
